This 1-day meal plan is designed for a 30-year-old male omnivore aiming for $\sim$\qty{2728}{\kcal} with balanced macronutrients (\qty{124.8}{\gram} protein, \qty{386.7}{\gram} carbohydrates, and \qty{75.8}{\gram} fat).

Key dietary choices:

\begin{itemize}
    \item High-quality, lean protein sources such as chicken breast, Atlantic cod, natural skyr, and semi-skimmed milk provide complete amino acid profiles to comfortably hit the \qty{124.8}{\gram} daily protein target while keeping saturated fats well below \qty{10}{\percent} of total energy.
\end{itemize}

Supplementation guidance:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Vitamin D: Like most adults residing in temperate European latitudes with limited year-round sun exposure, dietary sources alone (providing $\sim$\qty{1.3}{\micro\gram}) are insufficient to reach optimal physiological levels (\qtyrange{15}{20}{\micro\gram} / \numrange{600}{800} IU daily). Supplementation with vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) at \numrange{1000}{2000} IU (\qtyrange{25}{50}{\micro\gram}) daily is recommended, especially during autumn and winter months; confirm specific dosing with a healthcare professional based on 25(OH)D blood testing.
\end{itemize}
