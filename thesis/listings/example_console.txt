Rationale: This 1-day meal plan is designed for a 30-year-old male omnivore aiming for ~2,728 kcal 
with balanced macronutrients (124.8 g protein, 386.7 g carbohydrates, and 75.8 g fat). 

Key dietary choices:
- High-quality, lean protein sources such as chicken breast, Atlantic cod, natural skyr, and 
semi-skimmed milk provide complete amino acid profiles to comfortably hit the 124.8 g daily protein 
target while keeping saturated fats well below 10% of total energy.
- Complex carbohydrates from whole-grain rolled oats, potatoes, and rice noodles provide sustained 
glycogen replenishment and energy for physical activity.
- Healthy unsaturated fats are supplied via English walnuts, chia seeds, extra virgin olive oil, and
sesame oil, offering an optimal balance of ALA omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids to support 
cardiovascular health.
- Over 850 g of vegetables and fruits (apples, blueberries, banana, broccoli, carrots, bell peppers,
green beans) ensure rich dietary fiber (>54 g), abundant potassium (>6,000 mg), and ample vitamin C 
(>400 mg), comfortably exceeding all WHO/DGA micronutrient guardrails.
- Sodium is strictly controlled (~1,205 mg) through the use of low-sodium soy sauce and fresh herbs 
like dill and ginger instead of table salt.

Supplementation guidance:
- Vitamin D: Like most adults residing in temperate European latitudes with limited year-round sun 
exposure, dietary sources alone (providing ~1.3 µg) are insufficient to reach optimal physiological 
levels (15–20 µg / 600–800 IU daily). Supplementation with Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) at 
1,000–2,000 IU (25–50 µg) daily is recommended, especially during autumn and winter months; confirm 
specific dosing with a healthcare professional based on 25(OH)D blood testing.
- All other essential micronutrients (Calcium, Iron, Magnesium, Potassium, Zinc, Vitamin B-complex, 
and Vitamin C) are comprehensively covered through this whole-food omnivorous diet, so no routine 
multivitamin supplementation is warranted.
