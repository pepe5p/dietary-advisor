## USDA Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2020-2025 (paraphrased excerpts)

Section: core principles
The 2020-2025 Dietary Guidelines describe a healthy dietary pattern as one that emphasizes vegetables, fruits, whole grains, fat-free or low-fat dairy, lean protein foods, and oils, while limiting added sugars, saturated fat, sodium and alcoholic beverages.

Section: added sugars
Limit added sugars to less than 10 percent of calories per day starting at age 2; for an adult on a 2,000 kcal diet this corresponds to less than 50 g of added sugars per day.

Section: saturated fat
Limit saturated fat to less than 10 percent of calories per day starting at age 2; replace saturated fats with unsaturated fats, particularly polyunsaturated fats, when possible.

Section: sodium
Limit sodium intake to less than 2,300 mg per day for adults and children age 14 and older; lower limits apply to younger children.

Section: alcohol
Adults of legal drinking age can choose not to drink, or to drink in moderation by limiting intake to 2 drinks or less in a day for men and 1 drink or less in a day for women.

Section: vegetables and fruits
Healthy eating patterns include 2.5 cups of vegetables and 2 cups of fruit per day for a 2,000 kcal pattern, with emphasis on a variety of colours and food forms.
