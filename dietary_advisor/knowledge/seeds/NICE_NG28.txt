## NICE NG28 - Type 2 diabetes in adults (paraphrased excerpts)

Section: dietary advice
Encourage high-fibre, low-glycaemic-index sources of carbohydrate in the diet, such as fruit, vegetables, wholegrains and pulses; include low-fat dairy products and oily fish; and control the intake of foods containing saturated and trans fatty acids.

Section: weight management
For adults who are overweight, an initial body weight loss target of 5-10% should be discussed; this is associated with significant improvements in HbA1c and cardiovascular risk markers.

Section: alcohol
Discuss the increased risk of hypoglycaemia for people on insulin or sulfonylureas if alcohol is consumed without food, particularly in the evening.
