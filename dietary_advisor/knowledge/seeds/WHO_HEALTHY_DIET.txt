## WHO key facts on healthy diet (paraphrased excerpts)

Section: free sugars
Adults and children should reduce their intake of free sugars to less than 10% of total energy intake. A further reduction to below 5% of total energy intake (about 25 g per day for an adult) is suggested as a conditional recommendation for additional health benefits.

Section: salt / sodium
Adults should consume less than 5 g of salt (roughly 2 g of sodium) per day. Reducing salt intake is one of the most cost-effective measures countries can take to improve population health.

Section: fats
Total fat should make up no more than 30% of total energy intake. Saturated fats should be less than 10% of total energy intake and trans fats less than 1%, with a shift in fat consumption away from saturated fats and trans fats toward unsaturated fats.

Section: fruits and vegetables
At least 400 g (i.e. five portions) of fruit and vegetables a day - excluding potatoes, sweet potatoes, cassava and other starchy roots - reduces the risk of noncommunicable diseases and helps ensure adequate daily intake of dietary fibre.

Section: dietary fibre
Adults should consume at least 25 g of dietary fibre per day from whole grains, vegetables, fruits and legumes.
