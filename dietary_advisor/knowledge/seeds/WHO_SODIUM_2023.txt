## WHO global report on sodium intake reduction (2023) - paraphrased excerpts

Section: target intake
WHO recommends that adults consume less than 2,000 mg of sodium per day (equivalent to less than 5 g of salt per day). For children aged 2-15 years the recommended maximum should be adjusted downwards based on energy needs.

Section: cardiovascular impact
Reducing sodium intake to within recommended levels has been shown to lower blood pressure and reduce the incidence of cardiovascular disease, stroke, and coronary heart disease across populations.

Section: hidden sources
The majority of sodium in modern diets comes not from the salt shaker but from processed and ultra-processed foods, including bread, processed meats, sauces, cheese and ready meals; reformulation of these products is a key public-health lever.
