## NICE NG136 - Hypertension in adults (paraphrased excerpts)

Section: lifestyle interventions
Offer lifestyle advice to all people with suspected or diagnosed hypertension, including a healthy diet, regular exercise (150 minutes/week of moderate intensity), reduced alcohol intake, smoking cessation and reducing dietary sodium.

Section: dietary sodium
Recommend that adults aim to reduce their salt intake to less than 6 g per day (approximately 2.4 g sodium); for hypertensive patients, a target of less than 5 g salt per day (about 2 g sodium) should be considered.

Section: DASH-style diet
A diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and low-fat dairy with reduced saturated and total fat (DASH-style) can lower systolic blood pressure by 8-14 mmHg.
